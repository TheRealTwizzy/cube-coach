# Cube Coach: Scan a cube with the phone camera — plan

> Execution: native, TDD per unit, one fresh reviewer at the end, merge to `main` locally (standing rule), republish the Artifact.

## Context
Painting 54 stickers is slow. The user wants to scan their real mini cube (black body, stickers) with their phone. The page runs as a claude.ai Artifact, which **blocks live camera access** but allows file inputs, so the scan is **one photo per face** via `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="environment">` (phone camera; file picker on desktop). Everything is processed in the browser; photos are never uploaded or stored.

Decisions agreed in chat:
- 6 guided photos in the reading order and holds from `describe.READING` (F, R, B, L, U, D).
- Face finding: **A** sticker-grid finder (bright blobs separated by black gaps → best 3×3 lattice), **B** fallback black-body square, then 4 draggable corners (perspective-correct sampling) for the user to adjust.
- Colors: the 6 center samples are the references; each sticker is assigned in Lab space with **exactly 9 per color** (min-cost assignment); close calls are flagged "check this".
- Result fills the Solve net for review; the validator runs on Solve as usual.

## Units

### 1. `js/vision.js` (new, UMD `CubeVision`, pure, Node-tested)
Images are `{ width, height, data }` (RGBA `Uint8ClampedArray`, like `ImageData`).
- `rgbToLab([r, g, b]) → [L, a, b]` (sRGB D65).
- `homography(corners) → (u, v) => [x, y]` maps the unit square onto corners `[TL, TR, BR, BL]`.
- `findFace(img) → { corners, method: 'grid' | 'body' | 'none', confidence }`:
  - **grid:**
    1. Mark pixels as stickers if `V > 0.35 && (S > 0.3 || V > 0.6)`.
    2. Find 4-connected components; keep blobs with area 0.1–12% of the image, fill > 0.55 and bbox aspect 0.5–2.
    3. For each blob as a center:
       - Take the nearest similar-size neighbor as axis `u` and the nearest roughly perpendicular one (60–120°) as `v`.
       - Predict the 9 lattice points, match blobs within 0.35·|u|, and score the matches.
    4. Best with ≥ 7 matches → least-squares refine `c, u, v`. Orient so `u` points right and `v` down. Corners = `c ± 1.5u ± 1.5v`.
  - **body:** the largest dark (`V < 0.22`) component with a square-ish bbox; corners = its bbox.
  - **none:** a centered square, 60% of the short side.
- `sampleFace(img, corners) → 9 × { rgb, lab }`, row-major as seen. Per cell, take the per-channel median of a 5×5 sample grid in the middle half of the cell. Drop blown-out glare pixels (`V > 0.98 && S < 0.08`) unless most of the cell is like that.
- `classify(faces) → { colors: string[54], uncertain: number[] }`:
  - `faces` = `{ U: 9 samples, R: …, … }` in net orientation.
  - **Centers → color letters:** min-cost match of the 6 center Labs to a canonical palette (w y g b r o).
  - **Stickers:** Hungarian assignment of 54 stickers to 6×9 slots, with cost = weighted ΔE (L weight 0.5, since lighting varies between photos); each center is pinned to its own color.
  - **uncertain:** the assigned color isn't the nearest one, or the gap to the runner-up is under 8 ΔE.
- `quality(img) → { dark: bool, blurry: bool }`:
  - dark = mean V < 0.18
  - blurry = Laplacian variance under a threshold, on the downscaled image

### 2. `js/scan.js` (new, UMD `CubeScan`, pure, Node-tested)
- `createScan()`:
  - `order` = F R B L U D
  - `step()` → `{ index, face, instruction }` (instruction from `D.faceHint(face, SOLVED)`)
  - `setFace(face, samples)`, `redo(face)`, `done()`
  - `duplicateCenters()` → pairs of faces whose centers match (ΔE < 12), with a message: "Photos 2 and 4 both have a red center. Retake photo 4 showing the orange face."
  - `result()` → `classify(...)` mapped to a 54-sticker state + uncertain list

### 3. UI (`index.html`, `js/app.js`)
- **Scan my cube** button in the **My Cube** section, next to the palette. The scan panel (`#scan-panel`) replaces the painting section while scanning.
- The scan panel shows:
  - progress "Photo n of 6" and the hold instruction
  - a Take photo button (hidden file input with a label button)
  - a canvas with the photo and the 4 corner handles (pointer events, touch-friendly 44px hit areas)
  - the 9 read colors
  - Retake / Looks right / Cancel scan
  - a mini net filling in
- **Loading:** `createImageBitmap(file)`, which honors EXIF rotation. Draw at ≤ 480px long side; `getImageData`. Resample on each corner drag end.
- **Messages:**
  - no grid: "Couldn't find the face. Drag the 4 corners onto the face's corners."
  - dark or blurry: "Too dark to read…" / "The photo looks blurry…"
  - duplicate centers: the message from `duplicateCenters`
- **Finish:** fill My Cube (`setCube`, unconfirmed), outline uncertain stickers (reuse `.bad` styling as "check"), note "Scanned. Outlined stickers were uncertain; check them against your cube." Focus returns to the My Cube heading; the user reviews and presses "This is my cube".

## Tests (written first)
- **`test/imagegen.js` (test helper):** renders synthetic photos into RGBA:
  - background: gradient + random rectangles
  - a black rounded body placed by a homography (position, scale, rotation up to ±15°, mild perspective)
  - 9 stickers at given RGB with inset gaps
  - per-pixel noise, and an optional glare spot
- **vision:**
  - `rgbToLab` reference values
  - `homography` maps the unit corners exactly
  - `findFace` recovers corners within 4% of the face size on ≥ 18 of 20 random synthetic photos (seeded), including busy backgrounds, rotation and one glare spot
  - body fallback works when a sticker is missing (only 6 found)
  - `sampleFace` returns the drawn colors (ΔE < 6)
  - `classify` gets 54/54 on realistic mini-cube shades under 6 different lighting tints/exposures (orange near red, warm white vs yellow), with exactly 9 per color; flags a deliberately ambiguous sticker
  - `quality` flags a dark and a blurred image
- **scan:**
  - order and instructions
  - duplicate-center message names the right photos
  - `result()` maps faces to the correct sticker indices (checked with a scrambled state rendered to samples)
- **Browser:**
  - run the whole flow with 6 synthetic photos generated in the page
  - drag a corner and see it resample
  - phone-width layout, no console errors
- **Optional:** if the user drops real photos into `samples/`, tune thresholds and add them as a regression test.
